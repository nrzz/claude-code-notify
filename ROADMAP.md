# Roadmap

What could come next. None of it is promised; all of it is open to contributors. Comment on an issue or start a [discussion](https://github.com/nrzz/claude-code-notify/discussions) before a large change.

- Pushover and Gotify channels.
- A table of which terminals show OSC 9 and OSC 777 notifications, tested by volunteers on Konsole, GNOME Terminal and Terminal.app.
- Per-session mute (`claude-notify mute --session`).
